# Incident: 2026-04-07 14-40-20

## Summary

At 14:40 PM MST, the Pizza Creation Failure Alert fired through the discord webhook. By 14:44 PM MST, Pizza Creation Failure alert went back to normal. Communication between the jwt-pizza-service backend and the jwt pizza factory resulted in failures to successfully purchase pizzas. This major incident affected all users attempting to buy a pizza for approximately 5 minutes. The impact was seen by approximately 10-15 error logs that reported their was a failure attempting to connect to the jwt pizza factory.

## Detection

This incident was marked as pending at 14:38 and by 14:40 an alert had been sent out to my discord alerting me of the Pizza Creation Failure.

## Impact

This impacted every user attempting to complete an order between 14:38-14:44. All other services on the jwt-pizza-service backend were functional, orders were unable to be fulfilled at the jwt pizza factory.

## Timeline

All times are UTC.

- _14:38_ - Grafana Pizza Creation Alerts switches from normal to pending
- _14:40_ - Grafana Pizza Creation Alerts switches from pending to alerting. I am alerted via discord of a problem.
- _14:41_ - Grafana metrics confirm that pizza creation success is at 0 while pizza failure is high.
- _14:42_ - Grafana logs show 500 status codes on the api/order path to the jwt-pizza-factory. The logs have a link inside with a message to click the link to return functionality.
- _14:43_ - Link has been copied and box has been clicked to turn off the chaos monkey
- _14:44_ - Grafana Pizza Creation Alerts switches from alerting to normal. Incident has been resolved.

## Response

After recieving the alert, I responded to the error and resolved the issues as outlined in the timeline.

## Root cause

The root cause was a chaos monkey that had severed the ability to send orders to the jwt pizza factory for order completion

## Resolution

The incident was resolved by following the link and turning off the chaos monkey, thereby restoring the connection between the jwt-pizza-backend and the jwt-pizza-factory
